# Operational lesson candidate v1 — 29 October 2026

Status: **review note only; no active skill or permission policy changed**.

When an owner has approved one exact inert string-classification retry, retain
that approval with its action/scope identity and do not ask the same question
again merely because a session changed or context compacted. Classifying fixture
bytes is different from executing those bytes, installing a guard, changing
permissions, or starting a workload. A generic workload approval cannot fill a
missing guard-install approval.

Review quoted strings as data first, then identify whether any wrapper or inner
interpreter will execute them. POSIX argv equivalence needs an independent oracle;
shlex alone is not a full shell expansion or continuation parser. Required deny
results need assertions, including an absence/error policy, rather than printed
verdicts.

Keep one guard writer. Other sessions provide bounded findings and tests without
editing the same safety layer or running a denied retry. Track local parser
results separately from platform approval review: a local repair never supplies
permission to bypass an external denial. Record NOT_RUN and the actual blocking
layer instead of silently substituting a different execution channel.

Basis: sanitized FYP parser-review packet and patch, plus the owner's exact inert
retry approval forwarded for this review. This note is a proposed operational
lesson, not proof of autonomous learning, a promoted skill or an authority grant.
