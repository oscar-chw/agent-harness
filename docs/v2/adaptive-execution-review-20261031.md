# Adaptive execution: bounded historical review and candidate policy

31 October 2026. Design candidate only; no installed instructions, hook,
permission, model setting or execution engine was changed. This document does
not qualify reduced process overhead or token savings.

## Requirement

Support sophisticated workflows while choosing proportionate work for each
request. Complexity, uncertainty, consequence of error, reversibility and
continuation needs are separate inputs. A capable harness must also terminate
simple work directly. More tools, plans, agents or instructions are not a
quality metric.

## Candidate: choose a path, not a mandatory sequence

| Path | Typical trigger | Work permitted/needed | Evidence of completion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct | Definition, rewrite, explanation or supplied-evidence answer; no consequential unresolved uncertainty | Answer directly. No new plan, worker, persistent checkpoint or research merely to satisfy a template | Relevant source/content check where needed; no claim that nonexistent execution was tested |
| Bounded task | Local reversible change or investigation with limited dependencies | Inspect affected inputs, choose a minimal approach, execute and run the relevant check | Change-specific evidence; existing mandatory checks still apply |
| Rigorous task | Consequential effects, broad interfaces, difficult recovery, competing ownership or continuation | Durable plan/checkpoints, explicit authority, failure/recovery cases, appropriate independent review and integration gates | All applicable acceptance and release checks, bound to current revisions |
| Exploratory task | Material question whose answer/method is genuinely unknown | Bounded research/probes, competing hypotheses, explicit stopping condition; implementation only when justified | Supported conclusion, useful negative result or specific unresolved uncertainty |

These are execution choices, not a ladder every task must climb. Broad research
may be low-risk, and a one-line authentication change may be high-risk. A change
in scope can invalidate a route and require reassessment.

## Four independent controls

1. Reasoning depth: how difficult the inference is. A short answer may need deep
   reasoning. Do not change native model/effort settings mid-session or claim an
   effective model from a requested label.
2. Evidence acquisition: what missing information can change the decision. Use
   supplied/current evidence; perform required current-source verification when
   applicable. Do not search every store on every request.
3. Workflow machinery: dependencies, persistence, workers and handoffs. Create
   these when ownership, recovery or integration needs justify them.
4. Verification: strength of outcome evidence, determined by consequence and
   requirements. A small process cannot waive valid tests or authority gates.

## Proposed behavioral changes (not activated)

- Keep a tiny admission decision in the main reasoning process, not a new model
  call, routing service or mandatory form. Do not print route labels on every
  response; show them when they explain cost, risk, a hold or a material change.
- Required safety/access and acceptance checks remain applicable in every path.
  Other lifecycle stages are conditional; justify omitted work by its absence
  of relevance rather than fabricate a completed phase.
- Research only a consequential knowledge gap; stop repeated unchanged fetches
  or unavailable-tool calls. Respect current documentation/verification rules.
- Persist a graph for real dependencies/recovery/continuation needs. Do not
  mechanically expand each node into analysis/research/plan/test subnodes.
- Delegate bounded independent work when isolation, quality or elapsed-time
  benefit justifies total overhead and dispatch authority exists. No workers
  for routine lookups, ceremonial consensus or automatic self-review.
- Run checks appropriate to the change and required project gates. Expand
  verification for a discovered failure or unresolved risk; do not repeat a
  completed unchanged check merely to obtain another green line.
- Record material decisions/results once, by reference. Capture learning
  candidates from trustworthy corrections/outcomes; do not manufacture a skill
  or save full history for each answer.
- Stop on the defined outcome. A null result or no-change decision can complete
  an investigation; do not create work to fill a lifecycle template.

## Qualification before changing defaults

Freeze representative, authorized cases and the expected evidence requirements
before comparing current behavior with this policy. Use direct answers, small
fixes, ambiguous research, cold recovery and high-risk negative controls.

Required controls include a simple explanation that needs no tool, a current
fact that does need verification, a safe local fix, a one-line dangerous change,
a large read-only investigation, a stale source, a required failing gate and a
multi-session integration task. The candidate must not route dangerous work as
safe merely because it is short, or evade verification to reduce tokens.

Measure task correctness, required-check/authority violations, false escalation,
missed escalation, user corrections, total model tokens (including workers and
router), cached-token reporting, latency and process artifacts. Preserve the
existing quality-at-least-baseline and aggregate-tokens-at-most-baseline rules.
Also inspect per-task-class regressions; aggregate improvement cannot conceal a
broken urgent/high-risk class. Missing reliable usage data is UNKNOWN.

No paired native replay was launched for this review. Before promotion, reconcile
the conflicting trigger text, compare against the current/no-candidate behavior,
keep failed cases and independently review the change. Global defaults and
permission controls were not modified by this document.
