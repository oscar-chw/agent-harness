# Minimum sufficient checkpoint

3 November 2026. Guidance for node4, not a native actor checkpoint or verification.

## Preserve the decision, not the conversation

The owner wants one evidence-backed harness, continuity and little coordination
burden. Sufficient state lets an authorised cold reader identify the goal,
separate established from unresolved work, and take a bounded next action
without reconstructing the chat or guessing permission. Complete narrative is
unnecessary; lossless memory is not promised.

For material unfinished work, state explicitly:

- Fixed goal, current stage and constraints; latest decision-changing steering.
  Interruption does not silently cancel earlier work. A real stop overrides
  a stale plan.
- Decisive result and its subject: observed/reported/inferred/unknown, with
  PASS/FAIL/NOT_RUN. File creation, tool delivery and completed execution differ.
- Active correction, consequential uncertainty and authority/hold that can
  change the next action. Summaries neither grant access nor clear refusals.
- Next action, accountable owner, prerequisite and recorded remaining budget.
  Missing attempt/usage data is unknown, never a reset or zero.

Lead with these facts under interruption, not chronology. Preserve useful
independent work without making the reader repeat or discard it.

## Link depth; retain the consequential meaning

Link originals/spans, frozen inputs, full commands/logs, failures and detailed
comparisons through stable project paths and revision-bound receipts. Keep
the meaning of the active correction or hold explicit; link its audit trail.
“See history” alone cannot tell the reader whether a source changed or a gate ran.

A receipt identifies bytes, not current machine truth or action authority.
Revalidate relevant dependencies before acting. Preserve unrelated valid
receipts; mark a missing original as a gap. Following a link still requires
the original source/access boundary. Do not duplicate the whole corpus.

## Proportional use and limits

A one-shot answer may finish directly with material uncertainty; no checkpoint
is needed to demonstrate compliance. For continuation, field/byte limits can
reject bloat or omissions but cannot establish useful cold pickup. Root must
judge whether the reader preserves meaning and advances the task.

Only this new file is authored. Implementation, semantic/native verification
and general sixteen-case qualification remain main-owned and NOT_RUN.
