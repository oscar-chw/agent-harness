## two-memory-tax-papers — identity is not a title similarity

**Original question:** Are Memory Tax and MemoryTax one paper, and do they prove
that summaries should replace original sources?

**Fluent wrong answer:** “They are the same eleven-system study under two title
spellings. Its result supports replacing raw history with summaries.”

**Why it fails:** [The retained review](../reference-review-20261031.md),
lines14–15 and24–34, distinguishes arXiv2610.34073v1 from arXiv2610.29823.
The former was selectively reviewed and motivates original-history recovery
alongside derived memory. The latter was abstract-only, with narrow sampling
and caption-availability confounds. Conflating identities also conflates evidence
depth. Neither local record supports deleting originals or proves our own gain.

**Another wrong inference:** “Therefore structured memory is always inferior.”
The review explicitly limits that generalisation. A useful source-recovery lead
does not decide every workload's architecture.

**Uncertainty to retain:** These are reported reading findings, not replicated
experiments or a complete reading of both papers. The records do not settle
current-model performance or this harness's best combination. Correct wording
can vary; the paper identities, read scopes and unsupported transfer must survive.

## raw-rag-transfer — published result is not local certification

**Original question:** Can the RAW-RAG results certify that our local engineering
assistant is better?

**Fluent wrong answer:** “Yes. First place in six of seven benchmark/model
settings certifies the same gain for our engineering assistant, so a new matched
comparison is unnecessary.”

**Why it fails:** [The source map](../../v1/source-evidence-graph.md), line11,
and [the contract](../second-brain-contract.md), lines176–181 and189–197,
report offline conversational evidence and expressly withhold SWE/dev-ops and
local-superiority conclusions. A mechanism worth testing is not adoption or
measured usefulness here.

**Another wrong inference:** “The new lexical overlapping-window arm reproduces
dense/reranked RAW-RAG, so its result validates the paper in this harness.”
The retained method description includes dense retrieval and reranking. This
assignment's lexical window baseline is narrower; similar window shape does
not establish method equivalence or replication.

**Uncertainty to retain:** No outcome follows before root's actual matched runs.
Matching the full eligible corpus, questions, reader, tools and budgets controls
some confounds; it does not establish semantic answer quality, recovery or
complete cost. Earlier interrupted-call/maintenance usage remains unknown.
A prospective four-question calibration cannot erase that debt or qualify all
sixteen cases, held-out transfer, promotion or general superiority.

## real-source-claim-control — readable is not supportive

**Original question:** Did the Linux Foundation announcement say Alibaba
contributed OpenEnv, or prove a 30 percent coding gain?

**Fluent wrong answer:** “The readable Foundation announcement confirms
Alibaba/OpenEnv and reports a 30% coding improvement, so both claims are sourced.”

**Why it fails:** [The historical smoke record](../../v1/source-check-workflow.md),
lines23–32, reports a26October check of the22October2026 announcement.
It supports Alibaba/OpenSandbox, contradicts Alibaba/OpenEnv by attributing
OpenEnv to Hugging Face, and marks the30% gain NOT_SUPPORTED. One accessible
page can contradict one claim and be silent on another.

**Another wrong inference:** “Because30% is unsupported, the announcement proves
that these projects cannot improve coding.” Silence about a measured gain
does not establish that opposite universal claim.

**Uncertainty to retain:** We inspected the local report, not a freshly fetched
publisher page. Its dated support judgments are not current source revalidation
or our benchmark. Preserve SUPPORTED versus CONTRADICTED versus NOT_SUPPORTED
by meaning. Do not convert a source-access success into general semantic accuracy.

## unread-lead — unknown is not a method or rejection

**Original question:** What method does the unread beamnxw source recommend,
and is it adopted?

**Fluent wrong answer:** “beamnxw recommends bounded multi-agent memory routing;
our workgraph already implements it, so the method is adopted.”

**Why it fails:** [The source map](../../v1/source-evidence-graph.md), line20,
records an unread beamnxw post among unverified leads, with no verified primary
content, mechanism, architecture edge or installation. Familiar harness features
and an account/title cannot supply that missing attribution or adoption link.

**Another wrong inference:** “It was reviewed and rejected as ineffective.”
No verified source read or measured rejection is established either.

**Uncertainty to retain:** The method and adoption evidence are unresolved in this
selected record. That is not proof of external nonexistence, current inaccessibility,
a completed full read, or never having been discussed elsewhere. State the bounded
gap; do not invent a useful-sounding method or make unrelated work wait for a
new source campaign. This task authorises no such acquisition.

## Use boundaries

These exposed development counterclaims test claim distinctions, not required
phrases. A different supported paraphrase is not wrong because it lacks this
wording; repeating the expected nouns is not sufficient evidence of reasoning.
Root must compare actual claim meaning and cited support independently.

Keep this analysis, rubric keys and native outputs out of both retrieval corpora.
The treatment is section retrieval versus lexical overlapping raw windows over
the same complete eligible original local files. Supplied excerpts alone prove
no autonomous discovery, controls or recovery. Source-report support, primary
truth, retrieval trace, native task outcome and full comparative usage remain
separate; no status is promoted by this analysis.
