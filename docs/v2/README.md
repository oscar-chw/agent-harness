## How sessions cooperate

One orchestrator integrates four workers with disjoint write scopes. Instructions,
status and results live in a designated shared project location. Workers inspect
that location at start/resume and meaningful task boundaries. Messages are for
urgent intervention or newly authorized work, not routine confirmations. Idle
workers stop; this is not a model-powered heartbeat or a guarantee that a dormant
client wakes itself. Existing gates still decide completion.

The current five-session ownership map and shared instructions are in
`handoffs/v2-completion/{sessions.json,README.md}`. UI grouping is a convenience;
it does not create an execution boundary or synchronize client state.

## What self-learning must mean

1. Observe a real result or explicit correction, retaining scope and evidence.
2. Draft a candidate; distinguish an outcome sequence from an established cause.
3. Evaluate on new matched cases with independent grading and complete usage.
4. Promote only a justified improvement, retrieve it selectively, and withdraw
   stale or harmful advice without erasing history.

Capture, candidate preparation and correction mechanisms exist. The gate
observer has local source and installed-canary evidence; benefit and promotion
remain under qualification. A legacy command-only
sequence cannot prove that the acceptance check stayed unchanged. Native
procedure promotion remains held until actor/oracle and usage provenance are
actually qualified. Neither stored memory nor repeated use alone proves learning.

[Native draft preparation](methodology/native-preparation-20261106.md) now has an
observed one-call proof and source-bound preparation usage. Drafts remain proposed;
that result does not close the complete-cost or promotion gates.

## Before a final-v2 claim

Benchmark the exact final candidate after the fixes, not only an earlier build.
The [benchmark results](benchmarks/RESULTS.md) distinguish local checks, adapted
component measurements and official end-to-end scores. Diagnostic reruns of
cases already inspected are regression evidence, not a new held-out win.

Reconcile unread references honestly, qualify source-backed recall and correction,
test useful native task outcomes against baseline, and verify each installed
client/device. Keep quality at least baseline and aggregate tokens no greater
than baseline, including overhead and failed attempts. No universal superiority,
lossless memory, all-device parity or automatic promotion is established here.

Research packets and local coverage ledgers are integration inputs. Their private
provenance and publication eligibility need the final privacy gate; do not stage
or publish the entire working tree. The public package must remain portable.
